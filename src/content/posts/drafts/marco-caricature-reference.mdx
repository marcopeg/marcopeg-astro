These are the current character references for drawing me in blog covers and illustrations. Created on November 18, 2026; the older date keeps this draft at the bottom of the blog for easy reference.

**Same person, different roles.** Keep my short silver-gray hair, blue-gray eyes, rounded prescription glasses, and closely trimmed gray mustache and goatee. I'm 180 cm tall and weigh 92 kg: a comfortably fuller build, with natural head proportions.

> Coffee in hand, with delicate steam curling upward. Every version. Always.

Use the photos and these studies together when drawing a new scene. The rendering can adapt to the article, but the face and build should stay consistent.

## Everyday

Forest-green crewneck, dark trousers, relaxed posture, and a knowing smile. A natural-sized head and a soft midsection. This is the default version of me.

<Img src="/content/images/2026/11/marco-01-everyday-v4.jpg" alt="Everyday Marco: green sweater, silver hair, rounded glasses, and espresso in hand." />

## Nerd engineer

Navy overshirt, charcoal T-shirt, laptop, espresso, and a little rubber duck. I use the laptop's built-in keyboard or trackpad. **No external keyboard.**

<Img src="/content/images/2026/11/marco-02-engineer-v4.jpg" alt="Marco the engineer: working on a laptop with coffee in hand and a rubber duck on the desk." />

## Father

Striped T-shirt, navy baby carrier, a sleeping baby, and a quiet proud smile. One hand supports the carrier; the other holds the coffee away from the baby. The child is a generic illustration, not a portrait reference.

<Img src="/content/images/2026/11/marco-03-father-v4.jpg" alt="Marco the father: a sleeping baby in a navy carrier and an espresso held out to the side." />

## Adventurer

Weathered fedora, brown leather jacket, cream shirt, and a cross-body satchel. Indiana Jones / Han Solo energy, with my own face, glasses, gray hair, and fuller build. And the coffee, of course.

<Img src="/content/images/2026/11/marco-04-adventurer-v4.jpg" alt="Marco the adventurer: tipping a fedora while holding an espresso, wearing a leather jacket and satchel." />
