<p>To whom it may concern,</p><p>I’m Marco, and I’m writing this recommendation letter together with my wife, Lia. We are an Italian couple living in Sweden, and we initially met Annalisa five years ago, participating in the local International community. Since then, we have cultivated a strong friendship, and now we feel blessed to consider Annalisa and Ville as part of our close family.</p><p>As a couple, we tried to have a baby for some time. Due to Lia's medical conditions, we weren't sure we could have our biological child, so we also enrolled in the adoption adventure, but along the way, we were blessed with Mattia. Still, we had the time to get in touch with the adoption process and the needs of an adopted child, and we feel Ville and Annalisa have the sensibility, and the practical means, to provide a nurturing home to a lucky kid.</p><p>Annalisa shows a strong motherhood call and has been close to Lia for her entire pregnancy, supporting day-to-day matters and psychological sustenance during this life experience. Ville and I shared time learning together about my upcoming role as a father.
