<p>My direct manager is a good chap. He's the CEO of my company and comes from a top-performer consultancy company where people are apparently trained into optimizing processes – human and non-human – to the extreme.</p><p>He and his super-managers (that's how I call them) live by another famous quote that is often attributed to Peter Drucker:</p><blockquote><em><strong>What gets measured gets managed</strong></em></blockquote><p> That is the way of thinking that drives companies – and managers – crazy about KPIs, OKRs, and many other fancy acronyms around the concept of monitoring performances. Which way too often roots down to micro-managing people.</p>
