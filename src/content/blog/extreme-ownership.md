**Owning Leadership with SEAL Precision**

The elevator pitch for this book could be:

> There are no bad teams, only bad leaders.

It's catchy, and it's cheesy as well. The real deal is that everyone is at least the leader of their choices. Some of us are also leaders for others'.

Regardless of your current leadership situation, this book has a strong message for you:

> Leadership Is Ownership

You better dive into the pages to fully understand what "ownership" entails. But here are some examples:

*   Own your choices
*   Own your mistakes
*   Own your doubts
*   Own your body
*   Own your mind

To quote from the book: _"Leadership requires belief in the mission and unyielding perseverance to achieve victory, particularly when doubters question whether victory is even possible."_

If you're seeking a manual on leadership that challenges you to rise above, to be more than just another cog in the machine, and to lead with intent, purpose, and accountability, then this is a must-read.
