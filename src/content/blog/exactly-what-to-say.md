## The Magic Words for Influence and Impact

This book is highly practical and lists a few expressions that enhance your chances of getting things coming your way.

It's a manual. A "cut to the chase" content that proposes ways of phrasing common situation expressions and explains why that particular phrasing might work in your favor.

I will not spoil the contents, but my favorite _"magic word"_ is:

> What makes you say that?

Life is all about negotiations, and negotiation is often about turning rejection around. To quote the author: _"Success in negotiation is all about maintaining control in a conversation, and the person in control is always the person asking the questions."_

I often fall into the bad habit of answering objections with counterarguments, trying to prove the other person wrong. Working hard to make my points strong.

Do I need to say I seldom get through with it?
