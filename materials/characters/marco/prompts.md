# Marco character development

Built-in image generation. User-provided photographs establish the identity; later variants use the generated everyday character as a style and proportion reference as well.

## 1. Everyday Marco

Use case: illustration-story / identity-preserve character design.
Create ONE character design image of Marco, the SAME real adult man shown in all supplied reference photos. These photographs are identity references, not composition or background references. Use the frontal portrait and outdoor clear-glasses portrait most strongly; the sunglasses picture only informs the smile. Do not copy selfie distortion, browser/phone UI, lanyards, cars, buildings, logos or photo backgrounds.
Visual identity: fair skin, broad oval face with gently squared jaw, broad forehead with subtle expression lines, short straight silver-gray hair brushed up and slightly back into a compact textured quiff, slightly shorter sides, blue-gray eyes, rounded panto/browline prescription glasses with dark upper rims and thin translucent/metal lower rims and silver bridge. Medium straight nose with rounded tip, thin upper lip and fuller lower lip, short neatly trimmed silver-gray mustache and chin goatee, light cheek stubble rather than a full beard. Friendly observant eyes, small knowing smile. Preserve mature facial proportions and recognizable likeness. Do not turn him into a young brown-haired bearded stock developer, bald professor, huge-nosed clown, celebrity or plastic mannequin.
STYLE: sophisticated warm hand-painted editorial caricature, expressive ink contours and richly shaded painterly color, tactile paper texture, anatomically sound hands, slightly enlarged head (about 15%) and subtly expressive eyebrows and smile. Charming, witty, grounded, recognizable; not photorealistic and not glossy Pixar 3D. This is the visual baseline for a reusable consistent character series.
VARIANT 1 — EVERYDAY MARCO: relaxed three-quarter standing pose, framed from head to mid-thigh, both hands visible, forest-green crewneck sweater with small blank/no-logo chest, dark casual trousers, clear prescription glasses. One hand loosely in a pocket, the other making a small conversational open-palm gesture, relaxed approachable smile. Ordinary confident person, no costume.
COMPOSITION: 3:2 landscape character reference plate, a single character centered with generous breathing room, neutral warm ivory parchment background with a faint ground shadow only, soft warm directional light. Keep the whole hairstyle visible. No writing, captions, signatures, labels, panels or watermark.
