# Marco's four personas

All personas share the same mature face, silver hair, blue-gray eyes, round prescription glasses and close gray goatee. All use natural adult head proportions and Marco’s stated 180 cm / 92 kg, comfortably fuller build with a soft midsection. A coffee cup visibly held in a hand, with delicate aroma/steam curls visibly whirling upward from its surface, is mandatory in every persona, always; a cup resting on a desk is insufficient. Other props should support the article rather than accumulate into a checklist.

## 1. Everyday / normal guy

Forest-green crewneck or navy casual shirt, dark trousers, relaxed shoulders, one hand holding an espresso with curling steam and the other relaxed or in a pocket, warm knowing smile. Ordinary confidence. Default when an article needs Marco without a particular role. Aviator sunglasses are an optional outdoor variation when requested; they are not needed to make the character recognizable.

Prompt addition: “Everyday Marco in a forest-green crewneck, relaxed three-quarter pose and an espresso held in one hand. Friendly and observant. Preserve the established face and clear rounded glasses.”

## 2. Nerd engineer

Same glasses, slightly tousled silver quiff, navy rolled-sleeve shirt over a charcoal T-shirt. Curious focused expression or raised eyebrow. Espresso is always held in one hand; laptop and a rubber duck can carry the joke. Marco uses the laptop’s built-in keyboard/trackpad; do not add an external keyboard, which the user explicitly rejected as unlike him. Do not invent buck teeth, giant novelty glasses, a skinny teenage body or unkempt dark hair as shorthand for nerdiness.

Prompt addition: “Marco as a practical software engineer, navy rolled-sleeve overshirt, sleeves pushed up, one hand on the laptop’s built-in keyboard or trackpad and the other holding an espresso with curling steam; no external keyboard. Amused concentration, same face and glasses, warm editorial humor.”

## 3. Father

Same adult face and maturity; softer expression and relaxed protective posture. Cream-and-olive striped casual T-shirt with a navy fabric baby carrier is grounded in the supplied family photograph. Show a generic sleeping baby's back or side of head turned inward, with the head and body naturally supported. One adult hand supports the carrier, while the other holds the coffee out to the side, away from the baby. No need to reconstruct the child's likeness or make a new child identity reference. A family article may instead call for a hug or hand-holding; use only the family details supplied for that scene.

Prompt addition: “Marco as a father in a striped T-shirt, navy baby carrier, one hand gently supporting a sleeping baby turned toward his chest, the other holding an espresso with curling steam away from the baby. Warm quiet smile; preserve Marco's silver quiff, rounded clear glasses and gray goatee.”

## 4. Adventurer / Indiana Jones–Han Solo energy

Marco's own face, not Harrison Ford. Worn brown leather jacket, open-collar cream shirt, practical trousers, leather cross-body satchel and a weathered fedora. A wry raised eyebrow and a slightly daring stance evoke a resourceful pulp adventurer. Keep the clear prescription glasses and enough silver hair visible under the hat. Use a vest instead of the jacket for a stronger space-smuggler variation if requested. Fedora, jacket and satchel are the default combined interpretation. One hand can tip the hat; the other must hold an espresso. No weapon is needed.

Prompt addition: “Marco as a resourceful cinematic adventurer: worn brown leather jacket, cream open-collar shirt, weathered fedora and cross-body satchel. Wry smile, one hand tipping the hat and the other holding an espresso with curling steam. His own rounded glasses, silver hair and gray goatee remain unmistakable; do not borrow an actor's likeness.”
